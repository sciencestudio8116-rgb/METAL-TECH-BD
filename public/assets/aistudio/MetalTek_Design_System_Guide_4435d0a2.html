1. Color palette

4. Spacing and layout

1100px container, 12-column grid, 30px gutter, 8px spacing base. Section padding is 72px on desktop and 48px on mobile.

Spacing scale

Breakpoints

Desktop, above 1024px
3 columns, 30px gutter
Tablet, 769–1024px
2 columns
Mobile, 768px and below
1 column, full-width buttons, hamburger nav

Breakpoints follow Beaver Builder defaults (about 992px and 768px). Verify against the live site.
